Riffle is a free, open-source tool for the math of trading card games, and shows its work.

Riffle collects its own data every day: card details, prices from stores and price guides, and tournament decklists. Give it your collection and decks, and it shows what each deck lacks and what finishing it costs.

The code is public on GitHub, under the AGPL. Riffle is in development.

Riffle is an independent project by Brandon M. Keltz.

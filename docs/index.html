Riffle

What you own, what you're building, and what the gap costs, for the card games you play.

Riffle keeps your collection, your decks and every day's card prices on your own computer, and writes them into your notes: what each deck still needs, what it costs to finish, and a page behind every card you link.

What it does

  • Reads your collection from the app you scan cards with.
  • Keeps every day's prices from the stores and price sites, so no price day is lost.
  • Checks each deck against what you own and lists what to buy, cheapest first.
  • Writes it all into an Obsidian vault, with a note behind every card: its picture, text, price and the copies you own.

Where it is

Riffle is in development and runs from the command line. A public beta comes later. Magic: The Gathering first; more games follow.

The code is open source under the AGPL: github.com/keltzbm/riffle.
